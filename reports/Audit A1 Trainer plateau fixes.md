# Reset the noisy bar, then change the recipe

**Two fixes come before any new idea. First, the keep bar is set from a noise estimate that is too small: the A/A sd of 0.0018 comes from 3 runs, while the seed-1 vs seed-2 pairs of the three failed confirms give a pooled sd of about 0.0115 per run (3 df, computed from ar/results.tsv). The +0.01 bar is therefore about 1 sd, so "kept" mostly selects lucky seeds. Second, the 17 tweaks all sit in one family (loss and schedule constants), and the ideas.md items stay in it too, because all six act on the same PPO-plus-distillation loss. Beyond the six, the literature points to three levers that are not queued: a signal for prompt answers that does not depend on win/loss (search targets or a state-delta head), a source-of-prompt input, and a keep rule with a shared baseline pool. The cost below is about 3 GPU-hours for the cheap block. Every number labelled "est." is my arithmetic, not a measurement.**

## The bar sits at one sd, so lucky seeds get kept

Seed-1 gaps of 0.4815, 0.4829 and 0.4917 triggered confirms and all regressed at seed 2 (0.4750, 0.4636, 0.4723). That is the winner's curse expected when gaps of about 1 sd are selected. Binomial sampling over about 4,500 evaluation games gives sd about 0.0075 (est.), so evaluation games explain most of the noise. The rest (about 0.006, est.) is training variance and wall-clock variance: games per run range from 2,801 to 7,413. At sd 0.0115, detecting +0.015 with 80% power takes about **6 runs per arm** and +0.010 takes about **14** (est.). RL-comparison guidance agrees: pilot sds need n of 20 or more, and at N=5 a worked example missed the effect 51% of the time ([Colas et al. 2018](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1806.08295)). Engine-testing frameworks handle this with a sequential test on paired games. Fishtest-style SPRT stops when a log-likelihood ratio crosses a bound, and pairing the games saves about 17-20% ([Chessprogramming SPRT](https://www.chessprogramming.org/Sequential_Probability_Ratio_Test), [pentanomial](https://github.com/vdbergh/pentanomial)). Public Karpathy-style write-ups use plain keep-if-better with no noise handling (8 kept of 47 in one overnight run, [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/i-let-karpathys-autoresearch-agent-run-overnight)). The only noise-aware variant I found is a single paper that credits gains in units of a measured seed sd ([arXiv 2606.20394](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.20394), snippet only).

For ar/ this means a pooled baseline of 6 seeds, which every candidate is compared against (Welch, one-sided, mean of 3 seeds, extended to 6 if p is between 0.05 and 0.3). Stop reading single-seed gaps under 2 sd (0.023) as trends. Wu et al. also warn that 5-minute scores favour changes that help early ([Wu et al. 2018](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~mren/research/metaoptim/index.html)); the LR x6 row (-0.015) may be that bias.

## Prompt answers get no usable signal, and nobody has solved it for card games

I found no card-game paper that ablates sub-prompt learning, so the following is inference. Search skips prompts inside Trainers, so those answers get only PPO's noisy delayed signal. The start pilot's value head probably cannot tell a state with a played Trainer from one with a blank card (A2: the lists' Trainers tie a near-blank card), so the advantage of a Trainer play is about zero in expectation, and Energy has an immediate tempo effect. Lambda changes variance, not that blindness, so lambda 0.7 will likely give another +0.006-sized result. Factoring is not the lever: AlphaStar-style ordered heads with a pointer ([AlphaStar summary](https://www.aiwiki.ai/wiki/alphastar)) are already matched by separate prompt steps. Gumbel top-k with a completed-Q target scores low-probability moves and fixes the top-4 blind spot ([Danihelka et al. 2022](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2022/poster/6418)). Anchors (KL to the start, piKL, R-NaD) only cap the 0.474 to 0.469 drift and cannot add a skill ([piKL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.07544), [R-NaD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15378)). The C73-x1b oracle already tests whether answers can be improved at all. The experiments below start after its verdict or run beside it.

## A stuck agent needs a different proposal space, not a cleverer loop

Fork-and-Flush reports that 8 repeats of one agent ended anywhere from 68.3 to 100.0, and that more compute did not close the gap ([summary](https://explainx.ai/blog/fork-and-flush-idea-basins-autoresearch-agents-microsoft-2026), press figures only, paper not read). PACEvolve finds that agents persist with chosen ideas, and it fixes this with a capped idea pool plus a permanent failed-idea log ([arXiv 2601.10657](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10657)). AIRA-dojo shows the search policy matters little when the operators are poor ([arXiv 2507.02554](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02554v2)). ar/ already flushes context every session, so the missing parts are the failed-idea ledger and a rule that forces a structural change. Neither costs GPU. Both are program.md and ideas.md edits: a ledger of idea families (a family is dead after 2 tries with no gain beyond noise), and after 6 straight discards the next experiment must change the model, the practice distribution or the input interface, not a constant. Islands, MAP-Elites and parallel forks are not worth it at about 12 evaluations per hour.

## Conclusion

The ceiling matters. A2 shows the lists' Trainers only tie a blank card, so even perfect prompt answers may raise M by little, and the plateau could sit in Trainer valuation as much as in its inner choices. Items 2 and 5 together decide that: if the advantage of a Trainer play is about zero and the search spread at Trainer prompts is also about zero, stop spending runs on prompt-answer ideas and move to the practice distribution or the encoder. Until item 1 reports, no result from the current loop should be called a gain, kept or discarded.

Gaps: no card-game precedent for any item above; the pooled sd rests on 3 pairs; Fork-and-Flush, AlphaStar and SAC-Discrete figures come from secondary summaries; hyperparameter ranges for the KL and Gumbel items are untested.
